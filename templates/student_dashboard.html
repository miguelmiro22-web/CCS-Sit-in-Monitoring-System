📋 Laboratory Rules and Regulations

University of Cebu

College of Computer Studies

All students are required to strictly observe the following rules and regulations inside the laboratory:

  1. Maintain silence, proper decorum, and discipline inside the laboratory. Mobile phones and other personal electronic devices must be switched off or set to silent mode.
  2. Computer games and any non-academic activities are strictly prohibited inside the laboratory.
  3. Internet access is permitted only with the explicit permission of the instructor. Unauthorized downloading and installation of software is strictly prohibited.
  4. Access to websites unrelated to the current course or subject matter is not allowed.
  5. Deleting system files, reformatting storage media, or any act that may result in data loss is strictly prohibited.
  6. Students must observe their allotted computer time and are not permitted to stay beyond their designated schedule.
  7. Any vandalism, misuse, or damage to laboratory equipment will be the full financial responsibility of the offending student.

Violations of these rules may result in disciplinary action.
